Hi, I am Yuwei! I’m a third-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science at UCLA, advised by Prof. Eunice Jun; I also work closely with Prof. J.D. Zamfirescu-Pereira.

My research lies at the intersection of human–computer interaction (HCI), data visualization, and human–AI collaboration. I design and study interactive systems that help people (i) understand and communicate complex data and models, and (ii) interpret, steer, and work alongside AI.

I aspire to build collaborative experiences in which humans and AI stay in a tight loop, with human knowledge aligning AI as AI augments human intelligence.

I obtained my bachelor degree in Computer Science from Southern University of Science and Technology. Previously, I was fortunated to intern at Microsoft Research with Chenglong Wang, and work with Prof. Haojian Jin at UCSD.

⭐️ I’m seeking research internship opportunities! If you’d like to collaborate or chat about research, please feel free to reach out :)
